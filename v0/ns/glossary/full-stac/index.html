Definition
Terms from Mehl et al., 'A full-STAC remedy for global digital health transformation: open standards, technologies, architectures and content', Oxford Open Digital Health 2023;1:oqad018 (doi:10.1093/oodh/oqad018, PMC11932414), the paper the DPI-H RA draft cites for the full-STAC approach (sec-022, footnote 14). Definitions are the paper's own words, verbatim; nothing is paraphrased or chosen. Every term is a candidate until a person confirms it. The RA's own glossary (bean S1, smart-ra#133) depends on this scheme: where the RA uses a full-STAC term it links here with a SKOS mapping rather than copying the wording.
