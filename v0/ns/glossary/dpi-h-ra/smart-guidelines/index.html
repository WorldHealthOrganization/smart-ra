Definition
  • Standards-based, Machine-readable, Adaptive, Requirements-based, Testable Guidelines are a comprehensive set of reusable digital health components (e.g., interoperability standards, code libraries, algorithms, technical and operational specifications) that transform the guideline adaptation and implementation process to preserve fidelity and accelerate uptake. SMART Guidelines provide a five-step pathway to advance the adoption of best clinical and data practices, even if a country is not yet fully digital. (en)
  • Standards-based, Machine-readable, Adaptive, Requirements-based, Testable Guidelines هي مجموعة شاملة من مكونات الصحة الرقمية القابلة لإعادة الاستخدام (مثل معايير التشغيل البيني، ومكتبات الشفرات البرمجية، والخوارزميات، والمواصفات التقنية والتشغيلية) التي تُحدث تحولًا في عملية تكييف المبادئ التوجيهية وتنفيذها بما يحافظ على الأمانة لها ويسرّع اعتمادها. وتوفر SMART Guidelines مسارًا من خمس خطوات للنهوض باعتماد أفضل الممارسات السريرية وممارسات البيانات، حتى وإن لم يكن البلد قد أصبح رقميًا بالكامل بعد. (ar)
  • Las Standards-based, Machine-readable, Adaptive, Requirements-based, Testable Guidelines son un conjunto integral de componentes reutilizables de salud digital (por ejemplo, estándares de interoperabilidad, bibliotecas de código, algoritmos, especificaciones técnicas y operativas) que transforman el proceso de adaptación e implementación de las directrices para preservar su fidelidad y acelerar su adopción. Las SMART Guidelines ofrecen una vía de cinco pasos para avanzar en la adopción de las mejores prácticas clínicas y de datos, incluso si un país aún no está plenamente digitalizado. (es)
  • Les Standards-based, Machine-readable, Adaptive, Requirements-based, Testable Guidelines constituent un ensemble complet de composants de santé numérique réutilisables (par exemple, normes d'interopérabilité, bibliothèques de code, algorithmes, spécifications techniques et opérationnelles) qui transforment le processus d'adaptation et de mise en œuvre des lignes directrices afin d'en préserver la fidélité et d'en accélérer l'adoption. Les SMART Guidelines offrent un parcours en cinq étapes pour faire progresser l'adoption des meilleures pratiques cliniques et en matière de données, même si un pays n'est pas encore entièrement numérique. (fr)
  • Standards-based, Machine-readable, Adaptive, Requirements-based, Testable Guidelines представляют собой комплексный набор многократно используемых компонентов цифрового здравоохранения (например, стандартов интероперабельности, библиотек кода, алгоритмов, технических и операционных спецификаций), которые преобразуют процесс адаптации и внедрения руководств, чтобы сохранить точность их соблюдения и ускорить их внедрение. SMART Guidelines предлагают пятиэтапный путь для содействия внедрению передовой клинической практики и практики работы с данными, даже если страна еще не полностью перешла на цифровые технологии. (ru)
  • Standards-based, Machine-readable, Adaptive, Requirements-based, Testable Guidelines是一套全面的可重用数字卫生组件（例如互操作性标准、代码库、算法、技术和业务规范），可变革指南的适配和实施过程，以保持保真度并加快采纳。即使一个国家尚未完全实现数字化，SMART Guidelines也提供了推进最佳临床和数据实践采用的五步路径。 (zh)

The RA draft's own glossary wording, verbatim (folio/dpi-h-ra/glossary/t-glossary-lead-a31853.md). Substantive and wording alternatives across the DTH corpus (smart-ra#133 S1, smart-ra#139, smart-ra#140, smart-ra#142): (1) PHC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, p. 11) and SFP handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/page-011.md, p. 11): "Standards-based, Machine-readable, Adaptive, Requirements-based, and Testable Guidelines are recommended digital capabilities, health and data content aligned with normative recommendations. They represent packages of comprehensive documentation and reusable digital health components (e.g. health content, interoperability standards, code libraries, algorithms, technical and operational specifications) that facilitate country adaptation and adoption of clinical and public health protocols, health systems strengthening recommendations, and data and interoperability standards into digital health information systems with fidelity. SMART Guidelines serve as a starting point and as foundational specifications to advance the development of robust person-centred digital systems. These specifications are software-agnostic, context-agnostic, and are applicable to all levels of digital maturity (10,23)." (2) SC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-009.md, p. 9): "The WHO guidelines for developing Standards-based, Machine-readable, Adaptive, Requirements-based and Testable components for implementing digital health capabilities. The components include interoperability standards, code libraries, algorithms, and technical and operational specifications. The SMART Guidelines provide a five-step pathway to advance the adoption of best clinical and data practices, even if a country is not yet fully digital (12, 13)." (3) PC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240116191-eng/sections/page-009.md, p. 9): "WHO standards-based, machine-readable, adaptive, requirements-based, and testable Guidelines are recommended digital capabilities, health and data content. They represent a comprehensive set of reusable digital health components (e.g., interoperability standards, code libraries, algorithms, technical and operational specifications) that transform the guideline adaptation and implementation process to preserve fidelity and accelerate uptake. SMART Guidelines provide a five-step pathway to advance the adoption of best clinical and data practices, even if a country is not yet fully digital.11, 12" All are recorded as candidates; none is chosen.
