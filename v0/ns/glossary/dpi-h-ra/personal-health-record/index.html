Scope note
Described in Appendix C (Lifelong Health Record, Terminology and Scope) as the person-facing application (such as a Digital Health Wallet) through which individuals access, view, and contribute to their health data, distinct from the underlying LHR data infrastructure. A candidate: no standalone glossary definition has been written.
