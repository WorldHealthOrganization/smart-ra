Definition

  • Лицо, роль или организация, заинтересованные в результатах архитектуры. (ru)
  • 对架构成果有利益关系的个人、角色或组织。 (zh)

Scope note
The RA draft's own glossary wording, verbatim (folio/dpi-h-ra/glossary/t-glossary-lead-a31853.md). Substantive and wording alternatives across the corpus (smart-ra#133 S1, smart-ra#140): (1) RA Table 3.1 (library/who-dpi-h-reference-architecture-draft-v1/sections/sec-026-31-the-architecture-description-approach.md): "An individual, team, or organisation with interests in, or concerns relative to, the architecture." (2) SC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-009.md, p. 9): "In the context of digital health transformation, anyone who is affected by or interested in the consequences of the efforts, including the planning team, end users, beneficiaries and funders." (4) DIIG Annex 1.1 Glossary (who-iris/library/9789240010567-eng/sections/page-141.md, p. 129): "Any person who is affected by or interested in the consequences of the digital health implementation; stakeholders include the planning team, end-users, beneficiaries and funders." (Also enumerated in SFP handbook §1.1, who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/sec-009-11-analyse-key-stakeholders.md, page-020.md, p. 8.) All are recorded as candidates; none is chosen.
Example
A patient/ health service user, a health worker, a public-health agency, a regulator. (en)
