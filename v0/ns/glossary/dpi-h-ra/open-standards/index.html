Definition
  • Standards that are documented and made freely available to the public, which are developed, approved and maintained via a collaborative and consensus-driven process. Open standards facilitate interoperability and data exchange and are intended for widespread adoption (20). (en)
  • معايير موثقة ومتاحة مجانًا للجمهور، تُطوَّر وتُعتمد وتُصان من خلال عملية تعاونية قائمة على توافق الآراء. وتيسّر المعايير المفتوحة قابلية التشغيل البيني وتبادل البيانات، وهي مخصصة للاعتماد على نطاق واسع (20). (ar)
  • Estándares documentados y puestos a libre disposición del público, que se elaboran, aprueban y mantienen mediante un proceso colaborativo y basado en el consenso. Los estándares abiertos facilitan la interoperabilidad y el intercambio de datos y están concebidos para una adopción generalizada (20). (es)
  • Normes documentées et mises gratuitement à la disposition du public, qui sont élaborées, approuvées et tenues à jour au moyen d'un processus collaboratif et fondé sur le consensus. Les normes ouvertes facilitent l'interopérabilité et l'échange de données et sont destinées à une adoption à grande échelle (20). (fr)
  • Стандарты, которые задокументированы и находятся в свободном доступе для общественности и которые разрабатываются, утверждаются и поддерживаются в рамках совместного процесса, основанного на консенсусе. Открытые стандарты способствуют интероперабельности и обмену данными и предназначены для широкого внедрения (20). (ru)
  • 有文件记录并向公众免费提供的标准，通过协作和共识驱动的过程制定、批准和维护。开放标准促进互操作性和数据交换，旨在得到广泛采用 (20)。 (zh)

Scope note
PHC handbook glossary wording, verbatim (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, p. 10), identical (with minor punctuation/citation differences) in PC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240116191-eng/sections/page-008.md, p. 8) and SFP handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/page-010.md, p. 10), and near-identical (two sentences) in SC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-009.md, p. 9): "Standards that are documented and made freely available to the public. Open standards are developed, approved and maintained via a collaborative and consensus-driven process. Open standards facilitate interoperability and data exchange and are intended for widespread adoption (10)." Substantive/complementary alternative in RA §2.2.2 (library/who-dpi-h-reference-architecture-draft-v1/sections/sec-022-222-the-enabling-conditions-for-reddhi.md, p. 35–39), citing Mehl et al. 2023 (library/mehl-2023-full-stac-oqad018/, linked via exactMatch to glossary/full-stac.glossary.json): "Open standards are validated and vetted specifications, repeatedly reusableacross contexts, that address common requirements, processes, and interactions and enable fidelity metrics." All are recorded as candidates; none is chosen (smart-ra#133 S1, smart-ra#139, smart-ra#142 item 3, smart-ra#143).
