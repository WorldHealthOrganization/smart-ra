Scope note
Described in Appendix D.1 (Health Management Information System) as a widely deployed open-source health management information application providing aggregate reporting, indicator calculation, dashboards, and tracker modules. Shared with dth-hrh, dth-phc, and dth-sc. A candidate: no standalone glossary definition has been adopted.
