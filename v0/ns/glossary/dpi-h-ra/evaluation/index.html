Definition
  • The systematic and objective assessment of an ongoing or completed intervention with the aim of determining the fulfilment of objectives, efficiency, effectiveness, impact and sustainability (8). (en)
  • التقدير المنهجي والموضوعي لتدخل جارٍ أو مكتمل بهدف تحديد مدى تحقيق الأهداف والكفاءة والفعالية والأثر والاستدامة (8). (ar)
  • Valoración sistemática y objetiva de una intervención en curso o finalizada con el fin de determinar el cumplimiento de los objetivos, la eficiencia, la eficacia, el impacto y la sostenibilidad (8). (es)
  • Appréciation systématique et objective d'une intervention en cours ou achevée, visant à déterminer la réalisation des objectifs, l'efficience, l'efficacité, l'impact et la durabilité (8). (fr)
  • Систематическая и объективная оценка текущего или завершенного вмешательства с целью определения степени достижения целей, эффективности, результативности, воздействия и устойчивости (8). (ru)
  • 对正在进行或已完成的干预措施进行系统、客观的评估，旨在确定目标的实现情况、效率、效果、影响和可持续性 (8)。 (zh)

Scope note
SFP handbook glossary wording, verbatim (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, page-009.md, p. 9, citing Monitoring and evaluating digital health interventions, WHO 2016). Wording alternative in DIIG Annex 1.1 Glossary (who-iris/library/9789240010567-eng/sections/page-140.md, p. 128, citing WHO evaluation practice handbook, 2013): "The systematic assessment of an ongoing or completed intervention to determine whether the intervention is fulfilling its objectives and to demonstrate an effect on health outcomes." Also contrasted with monitoring in SFP §6.1 (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/sec-029-61-develop-a-monitoring-and-evaluation-plan.md, page-051.md, p. 39, quoting Pritchett et al. 2013): "Evaluation asks whether the project is doing the right things, while monitoring asks whether the project is doing things right." Both definitions are recorded as candidates; neither is chosen (smart-ra#133 S1, dth-sfp#1 §2.1 Cluster B1).
