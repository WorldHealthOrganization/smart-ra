registry (en)
Definition
  • A governed, authoritative and centralized information system that captures, stores and maintains the unique attributes and identifiers of health facilities, health service users, health products and/or the health workforce using a predefined canonical minimum data set. (en)
  • نظام معلومات مركزي خاضع للحوكمة ومرجعي، يلتقط ويخزن ويحفظ الخصائص الفريدة والمعرّفات الخاصة بالمرافق الصحية ومستخدمي الخدمات الصحية والمنتجات الصحية و/أو القوى العاملة الصحية، باستخدام مجموعة بيانات دنيا مرجعية محددة مسبقًا. (ar)
  • Sistema de información gobernado, autorizado y centralizado que recopila, almacena y mantiene los atributos e identificadores únicos de los establecimientos de salud, los usuarios de los servicios de salud, los productos de salud y/o el personal de salud utilizando un conjunto mínimo de datos canónico predefinido. (es)
  • Système d'information gouverné, faisant autorité et centralisé, qui saisit, stocke et tient à jour les attributs et identifiants uniques des établissements de santé, des usagers des services de santé, des produits de santé et/ou des personnels de santé à l'aide d'un ensemble minimal de données canonique prédéfini. (fr)
  • Управляемая, авторитетная и централизованная информационная система, которая фиксирует, хранит и поддерживает уникальные атрибуты и идентификаторы медицинских учреждений, пользователей услуг здравоохранения, продукции медицинского назначения и/или кадров здравоохранения с использованием заранее определенного канонического минимального набора данных. (ru)
  • 一种受治理的、权威的集中式信息系统，使用预定义的规范最小数据集，采集、存储和维护卫生机构、卫生服务使用者、卫生产品和/或卫生人力的唯一属性和标识符。 (zh)

Near-identical wording alternatives across the DTH corpus (smart-ra#133 S1, smart-ra#139, smart-ra#142): (1) PHC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, p. 11): "A governed, authoritative and centralized information system that captures, stores and maintains the unique attributes and identifiers of health-care facilities, health service users, products and/or health workforce using a predefined canonical minimum data set." (2) SC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-009.md, p. 9): "A governed, authoritative and centralized information system that captures, stores and maintains the unique attributes and identifiers of health facilities, health service users, health products and/or the health workforce using a predefined canonical minimum data set." (3) PC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240116191-eng/sections/page-009.md, p. 9): "A governed, authoritative and centralized information system that captures, stores, and maintains the unique attributes and identifiers of health facilities, health service users, products and/or health workforce using a predefined canonical minimum data set." (4) SFP handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/page-010.md, p. 10, truncated in source PDF): "A governed, authoritative and centralized information system that captures, stores and maintains the unique attributes and identifiers of health facilities, health service users, products and/or health workforce using predefined canonical minimum data etc." All five are recorded as candidates; none is chosen.
