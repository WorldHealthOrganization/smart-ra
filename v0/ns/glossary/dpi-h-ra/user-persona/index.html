Definition
  • A generic or archetypical profile of an end-user that is representative of a single type of end-user. (en)
  • ملف تعريفي عام أو نموذجي للمستخدم النهائي يمثل نوعًا واحدًا من المستخدمين النهائيين. (ar)
  • Perfil genérico o arquetípico de un usuario final que es representativo de un único tipo de usuario final. (es)
  • Profil générique ou archétypal d'un utilisateur final, représentatif d'un type unique d'utilisateur final. (fr)
  • Обобщенный или архетипический профиль конечного пользователя, представляющий отдельный тип конечного пользователя. (ru)
  • 代表某一类最终用户的通用或原型化最终用户画像。 (zh)

Scope note
PHC handbook glossary wording under 'User persona', verbatim (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, p. 11). Internal and cross-DTH alternatives (smart-ra#133 S1): (1) PHC handbook §3.2.1 body (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-013-32-get-to-know-the-users.md, pp. 33-35): "A user persona is a generic depiction of a relevant stakeholder, or end-user of the system who would be interacting with the PCPOSS." (2) SFP handbook glossary under 'Persona' (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/page-010.md, p. 10): "A generic aggregate description of a person involved in or benefiting from a health programme." All three are recorded as candidates; none is chosen.
