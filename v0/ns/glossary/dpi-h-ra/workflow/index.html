Definition

  • Enchaînement des activités d'une tâche à une autre (voir la section 3.3). (fr)
  • Последовательность действий при переходе от одной задачи к другой (см. раздел 3.3). (ru)
  • 从一项任务到另一项任务的活动序列（见第3.3节）。 (zh)

Scope note
PHC handbook glossary wording, verbatim (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, p. 11). Internal and cross-DTH alternatives (smart-ra#133 S1): (1) PHC handbook §3.3 body (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-014-33-map-business-processes-and-workflows.md, pp. 35-39): "Workflows are a visual representation of the progression of activities (tasks, events, interactions) performed within a business process (45)." (2) SFP handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/page-011.md, p. 11, citing DIIG/DAK): "A visual representation of the progression of activities (tasks, events, decision points) in a logical flow illustrating the interactions within the business process (21)." (PHC glossary defines a workflow as the sequence of activities itself; PHC §3.3 and SFP glossary define it as the visual representation of that progression.) All are recorded as candidates; none is chosen.
