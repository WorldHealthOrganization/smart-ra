digital health (en)
Definition
  • The systematic application of information and communications technologies, computer science, and data to support informed decision-making by individuals, the health workforce, and health systems, to strengthen resilience to disease and improve health and wellness. (en)
  • التطبيق المنهجي لتكنولوجيات المعلومات والاتصالات وعلوم الحاسوب والبيانات لدعم اتخاذ القرارات المستنيرة من جانب الأفراد والقوى العاملة الصحية والنظم الصحية، من أجل تعزيز القدرة على الصمود في مواجهة الأمراض وتحسين الصحة والعافية. (ar)
  • Aplicación sistemática de las tecnologías de la información y las comunicaciones, la informática y los datos para apoyar la toma de decisiones informada por parte de las personas, el personal de salud y los sistemas de salud, con el fin de reforzar la resiliencia frente a las enfermedades y mejorar la salud y el bienestar. (es)
  • Application systématique des technologies de l'information et de la communication, de l'informatique et des données pour appuyer une prise de décision éclairée par les individus, les personnels de santé et les systèmes de santé, afin de renforcer la résilience face aux maladies et d'améliorer la santé et le bien-être. (fr)
  • Систематическое применение информационно-коммуникационных технологий, компьютерных наук и данных для поддержки принятия обоснованных решений отдельными людьми, работниками здравоохранения и системами здравоохранения в целях повышения устойчивости к болезням и улучшения здоровья и благополучия. (ru)
  • 系统地应用信息和通信技术、计算机科学和数据，以支持个人、卫生人力和卫生系统作出知情决策，从而增强对疾病的抵御能力，改善健康和福祉。 (zh)

Scope note
The RA draft's own glossary wording, verbatim (folio/dpi-h-ra/glossary/t-glossary-lead-a31853.md). Substantive and wording alternatives across the DTH corpus (smart-ra#133 S1, smart-ra#140): (1) PHC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, p. 8, citing WHO Global Strategy on Digital Health 2020–2025): "Digital health is the systematic application of information and communications technologies, computer science, and data to support informed decision-making by individuals, the health workforce, and health systems, to strengthen resilience to disease and improve health and wellness (11)." (2) SC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-008.md, p. 8, citing DIIG and Global Strategy): "The systematic application of information and communications technologies, computer science and data to support informed decision-making by individuals, the health workforce and health systems, to strengthen resilience to disease and improve health and wellness (1, 2)." (3) SC handbook Introduction (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-012.md, p. 12, quoting the WHO Global Strategy on Digital Health 2020–2025, same source cited by dth-hrh): "the field of knowledge and practice associated with the development and use of digital technologies to improve health". All are recorded as candidates; none is chosen.
