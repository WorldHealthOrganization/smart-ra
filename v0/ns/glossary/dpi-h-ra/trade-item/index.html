Definition
  • Trade items are manufacturer-specific branded health products that are identifiable and unique to a particular brand or manufacturer. (en)
  • الأصناف التجارية هي منتجات صحية ذات علامة تجارية خاصة بالجهة المصنّعة، يمكن تحديدها وتكون فريدة لعلامة تجارية أو جهة مصنّعة بعينها. (ar)
  • Los artículos comerciales son productos de salud de marca específicos de un fabricante, identificables y exclusivos de una marca o un fabricante determinados. (es)
  • Les articles commerciaux sont des produits de santé de marque propres à un fabricant, identifiables et spécifiques à une marque ou à un fabricant donnés. (fr)
  • Торговые единицы — это специфичная для производителя фирменная продукция медицинского назначения, которая идентифицируема и уникальна для конкретного бренда или производителя. (ru)
  • 贸易项目是制造商特定的品牌卫生产品，可识别且为特定品牌或制造商所独有。 (zh)

Scope note
Owner decision 2026-10-31 (smart-ra#142 item 1, smart-ra#139): Trade Item is the shared WHO concept with this narrow definition from the Product Catalogue handbook Ch. 2 body (who-iris/library/9789240116191-eng/sections/page-028.md, p. 28). The GS1 trade item is a separate external concept and the WHO term's broadMatch (every WHO Trade Item is a GS1 trade item, not the reverse: GS1's also covers services). The PC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240116191-eng/sections/page-009.md, p. 9) and SFP handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, page-011.md, p. 11) print the GS1 wording — "Products or services that are priced, ordered or invoiced at any point in the supply chain" — citing GS1 (PC ref. 13, SFP ref. 19); that sentence is recorded here as a note citing GS1, not as the WHO concept's definition.
