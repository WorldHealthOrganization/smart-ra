features (en)
Definition
  • The characteristics of a system that include look and feel as well as what the system can do (e.g. “reporting” can be a feature of a PCPOSS). (en)
  • سمات النظام التي تشمل الشكل والمظهر وكذلك ما يمكن للنظام القيام به (على سبيل المثال، يمكن أن يكون «الإبلاغ» خاصية من خصائص PCPOSS). (ar)
  • Rasgos de un sistema que incluyen su aspecto y su manejo, así como lo que el sistema puede hacer (por ejemplo, la «generación de informes» puede ser una característica de un PCPOSS). (es)
  • Caractéristiques d'un système, qui comprennent son aspect et sa convivialité ainsi que ce que le système peut faire (par exemple, la « production de rapports » peut être une fonctionnalité d'un PCPOSS). (fr)
  • Характеристики системы, включающие как внешний вид и удобство использования, так и то, что система способна делать (например, «отчетность» может быть свойством PCPOSS). (ru)
  • 系统的特性，包括外观和感受以及系统能够做什么（例如，“报告”可以是PCPOSS的一项特性）。 (zh)

Scope note
PHC handbook glossary wording, verbatim (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, p. 9). Wording alternatives across the DTH corpus, differing only in domain example and 'a system' vs 'your system' (smart-ra#133 S1): (1) SC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-008.md, p. 8): "The characteristics of a system that include look and feel as well as what the system can do (e.g., “order processing” can be a feature of an order management solution)." (2) PC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240116191-eng/sections/page-008.md, p. 8) and SFP handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/page-009.md, p. 9): "The characteristics of your system that include look and feel as well as what the system can do (e.g., “approval workflow” can be a feature of a product master data management solution)." All are recorded as candidates; none is chosen.
