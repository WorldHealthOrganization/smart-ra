Definition
  • A concrete offering, bundling services and data, that is made available to stakeholders. (en)
  • عرض ملموس يجمع بين الخدمات والبيانات، ويُتاح لأصحاب المصلحة. (ar)
  • Oferta concreta, que agrupa servicios y datos, que se pone a disposición de las partes interesadas. (es)
  • Offre concrète, regroupant des services et des données, mise à la disposition des parties prenantes. (fr)
  • Конкретное предложение, объединяющее сервисы и данные, которое предоставляется заинтересованным сторонам. (ru)
  • 一种捆绑服务和数据、向利益攸关方提供的具体提供物。 (zh)

Scope note
The RA draft's own glossary wording, verbatim (folio/dpi-h-ra/glossary/t-glossary-lead-a31853.md). Wording alternative in RA Table 3.1 (library/who-dpi-h-reference-architecture-draft-v1/sections/sec-026-31-the-architecture-description-approach.md): "A coherent offering that bundles services and data for use by stakeholders." Note: in the PC (9789240116191-eng) and SFP (9789240120747-eng) handbooks, the bare word 'product' is used as a synonym for `generic-product` (a health product / medicine); per owner decision 2026-10-31 (smart-ra#142), `product-master-data` and `generic-product` are separate concepts and no homonym split is needed on Product Master Data.
Example
A digital health wallet. (en)
