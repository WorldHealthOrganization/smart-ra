Consent Management Dashboard ()
This product is a user-facing interface that allows patients to manage their data sharing preferences and review who has accessed their records. It provides a transparent view of the consent lifecycle. The purpose is to give patients tangible control over their privacy and ensure they are informed about their data usage. It is a key tool for driving patient engagement and agency in the digital health system. Example: A web-based "Privacy Manager" where patients can revoke a specific hospital’s access to their past prescriptions.
