Medium-term expenditure projections produced. ()
These are three-to-five-year financial forecasts that estimate the future costs of health services, workforce, and infrastructure. They provide a strategic outlook that extends beyond the immediate annual budget cycle to support long-term planning. They allow for sustainable fiscal planning and the early identification of future funding requirements. This forward-looking approach helps prevent sudden budgetary shocks and supports the planning of large-scale national health initiatives. Example: A five-year projection showing the expected long-term costs of sustaining a new universal primary healthcare rollout.
