Evidence base for policy, accreditation, and payment reforms linked to quality ()
This outcome generates reliable evidence and performance data to support healthcare policies, accreditation programs, and value-based payment reforms. It provides objective insights into healthcare quality and system performance. The purpose is to support informed policymaking, regulatory oversight, and financing reforms tied to healthcare quality outcomes. It also strengthens accountability and performance-based healthcare governance. Example: A national insurance program adjusts reimbursement rates based on hospital quality performance indicators.
