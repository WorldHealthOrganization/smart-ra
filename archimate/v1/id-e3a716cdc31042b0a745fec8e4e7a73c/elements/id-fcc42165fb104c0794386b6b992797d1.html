Improved team accountability (traceable communications, clear task handovers). ()
This outcome creates a documented, auditable record of all communications, task assignments, and handovers, making it clear who communicated what, to whom, and when. It ensures responsibilities are explicitly assigned and follow-through can be verified. The purpose of this outcome is to foster accountability within care teams by making communication trails visible and reviewable. It also supports incident investigation and compliance with regulatory or institutional standards. Example: Following a patient complaint about a missed follow-up, a quality officer reviews the communication log and identifies that a referral task was assigned but never acknowledged. This enables the team to address the gap and prevent recurrence.
