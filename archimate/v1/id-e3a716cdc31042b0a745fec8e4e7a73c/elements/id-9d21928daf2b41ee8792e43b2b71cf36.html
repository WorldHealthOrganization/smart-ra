Budget planning, execution and oversight (monitoring): Ensure health budgets are planned, executed and monitored to support efficient budget execution, transparency and providers paid on time. ()
A strategic financial goal focused on the systematic formulation, distribution, and rigorous tracking of public health fiscal resources throughout their entire lifecycle. Its primary purpose is to ensure that healthcare funds are strategically allocated to high-priority initiatives, spent in strict compliance with fiscal regulations, and continuously monitored to maximize operational efficiency and eliminate waste. Example: By utilizing centralized financial software, a health department can precisely project the costs of a nationwide immunization campaign, authorize the secure release of funds to regional clinics, and audit real-time spending to ensure emergency resources are not being depleted prematurely.
