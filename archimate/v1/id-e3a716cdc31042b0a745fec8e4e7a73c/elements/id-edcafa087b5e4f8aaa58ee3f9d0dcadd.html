Population has verifiable digital health records ()
This outcome is achieved when the majority of citizens are issued secure, digital health credentials that are recognized across the country. It signifies the transition from paper-based or fragmented records to a trusted national digital infrastructure. The purpose is to provide a foundation for modern, data-driven healthcare that is person-centric and portable. It enables the systemic benefits of improved data quality, better care coordination, and citizen empowerment. Example: 90% of the population possessing a digital health record that is recognized by all public and private providers.
