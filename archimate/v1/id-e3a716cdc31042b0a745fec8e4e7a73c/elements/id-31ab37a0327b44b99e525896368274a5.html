Patient and family engagement ()
This sub-goal enables healthcare systems to actively involve patients and their families in care planning, decision-making, and self-management through digital tools and structured communication channels. The purpose of this sub-goal is to improve adherence, trust, and satisfaction by ensuring that patients and families are informed, respected, and empowered throughout their care journey. It also reduces miscommunication and supports better health outcomes by making patients active partners rather than passive recipients of care. Example: A patient managing a chronic condition uses a mobile health portal to review their care plan, report symptoms, and message their care team. Their family caregiver is granted controlled access to monitor progress and receive updates between clinic visits.
