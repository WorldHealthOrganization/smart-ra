Health Workforce Management ()
The organizational capability to strategically plan, deploy, and monitor the entire healthcare staff across various shifts and departments. It encompasses everything from scheduling and credentials tracking to ensuring the right clinical skills are available where they are needed most. It optimizes labor costs, prevents employee burnout, and guarantees that the hospital remains adequately staffed to provide safe patient care. Additionally, it ensures that all hours worked are accurately recorded and aligned with labor laws for seamless payroll processing. Example: "The hospital's centralized system for managing nurse rotations, tracking physician on-call schedules, and monitoring temporary staffing needs across all clinical wards."
