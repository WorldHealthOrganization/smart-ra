Governance, Policy, and Legislation ()
The strategic operational capability to establish, enforce, and adapt the legal frameworks, structural rules, and compliance standards that oversee a digital ecosystem. This capability ensures that all institutional strategies, digital platforms, and data exchanges are explicitly backed by binding legislation, clear accountability structures, and formalized organizational policies. Possessing this capability eliminates regulatory ambiguity, mitigates legal risks, and builds public and institutional trust in digital systems. For leadership, it provides the mandatory mandate and oversight required to enforce data privacy compliance, secure long-term public funding, and align multi-sector stakeholders under a unified digital roadmap. Example: "The national Governance, Policy, and Legislation capability that enabled the drafting and passage of the Digital Health Act, legally enforcing the adoption of standard registries and securing the statutory budget for national data infrastructure."
