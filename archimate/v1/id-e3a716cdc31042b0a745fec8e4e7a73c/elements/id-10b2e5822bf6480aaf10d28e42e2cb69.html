Privacy / Consent/ Trust ()
The core operational capability to enforce data confidentiality, capture and respect individual data-sharing preferences, and maintain absolute security across all digital interactions. This capability ensures that a user's personal information—whether patient medical records or employee financial data—is processed strictly in accordance with legal mandates and explicit permissions. Possessing this capability eliminates the risk of unauthorized data leaks, regulatory non-compliance fines, and systemic breaches that damage institutional reputation. For leadership, it creates a secure foundation that encourages widespread user adoption, ensuring that citizens and staff confidently trust digital platforms with their most sensitive personal and professional data. Example: "The Privacy, Consent, and Trust capability that automatically applies a patient's digital consent directives across the network, instantly blocking unauthorized clinics from accessing their medical history without explicit, time-bound permission."
