Eligibility algorithm ()
The eligibility algorithm is a set of logical rules used to instantly determine if a person is covered for a specific medical service. it analyzes patient data, policy rules, and current fund status to provide a "Yes/No" decision at the point of care. Its purpose is to provide real-time financial certainty for both patients and healthcare providers. It ensures that clinical services are only provided to eligible members and that the correct co-pays or subsidies are applied. Example: A logic engine that confirms a patient is currently active in the national fund and eligible for free maternity care.
