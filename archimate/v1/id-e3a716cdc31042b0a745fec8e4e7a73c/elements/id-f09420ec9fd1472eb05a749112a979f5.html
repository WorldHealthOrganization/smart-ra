Civil Society ()
Civil society includes non-governmental organizations, advocacy groups, and the general public who monitor the performance and fairness of the health system. They represent the voice of the taxpayer and the patient in health financing discussions. The purpose is to have visibility into health financing trends and ensure that public funds are being used for their intended purposes. They value transparency, accountability, and the equitable distribution of health resources. Example: An anti-corruption NGO using open data to verify that funds for rural clinics were actually disbursed as planned.
