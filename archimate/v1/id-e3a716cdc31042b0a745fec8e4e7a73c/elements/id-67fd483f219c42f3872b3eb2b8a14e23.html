Scheme-level fund management ()
This capability involves the high-level administration of pooled health resources at the level of the specific insurance or coverage scheme. It manages the flow of money between collection, investment, and disbursement functions to ensure long-term solvency. The objective is to protect the financial stability of the health fund and ensure it can meet its future obligations to beneficiaries. It provides the financial oversight needed to manage risk-sharing across the entire insured population. Example: A national health fund manager reallocating resources to cover an unexpected spike in regional hospitalization costs.
