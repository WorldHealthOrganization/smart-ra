Clinical decision support integration ()
This sub-goal enables healthcare systems to embed evidence-based recommendations directly into clinical workflows and digital platforms. It allows healthcare providers to receive real-time guidance and alerts while delivering patient care. The purpose of this sub-goal is to imprve the consistency, quality, and safety of clinical decisions across healthcare facilities. It also reduces errors, delays, and unnecessary variations in diagnoses and treatment. Example: A hospital electronic medical record system automatically alerts physicians when a prescribed medication may conflict with a patient’s allergy history.
