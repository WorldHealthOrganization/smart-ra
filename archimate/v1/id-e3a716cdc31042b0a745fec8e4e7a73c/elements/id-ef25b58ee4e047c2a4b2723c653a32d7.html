A Central Health Workforce Registry ()
The long-term strategic objective of establishing a unified, authoritative national system that houses and manages the data of all healthcare workers across the entire health system. This milestone aligns government, public health facilities, and private institutions under a single governance and operational standard for workforce data. Achieving this goal eliminates fragmented data silos, prevents credential fraud, and provides a clear picture of workforce distribution nationwide. For the organization, it establishes a foundational source of truth that guarantees payroll accuracy, simplifies licensing verification, and enables precise, data-driven workforce planning. Example: "The national mandate to successfully launch and populate a Central Workforce Registry by Q4, onboarding all 150,000 public and private healthcare practitioners onto a single verified tracking platform."
