Continuity of Care Bundle ()
Continuity of Care Bundle: A structured, interoperable digital package (e.g., FHIR-based) including discharge summary, medication list, pending results, and follow-up plan. Reduced readmission rates, duplicated tests and adverse events due to missed information. Improved provider accountability across care episodes. Patient-facing discharge/transition plan (simplified language, reminders). Population-level monitoring of transition-of-care quality indicators (e.g., % timely discharge summaries sent, 30-day readmissions).
