DPI-H: Facility Registry ()
The self-contained software component within the national architecture that serves as the single, authoritative source of truth for identifying and authenticating all healthcare facilities. It maintains a secure, real-time database of unique facility IDs, geographic locations, operational statuses, and ownership details. It eliminates data duplication and confusion across disconnected government and hospital systems by providing a standardized registry interface. This consistent identification ensures that labor costs, resource distributions, and payroll allocations are mapped accurately to the exact facility where the work took place. Example: "The DPI-H Facility Registry module that validates a clinic's unique national identifier before authorizing local staff payroll distributions and regional budget allocations."
