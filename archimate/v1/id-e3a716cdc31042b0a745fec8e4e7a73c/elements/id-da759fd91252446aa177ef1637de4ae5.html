Allocation scenario modelling tools ()
This service allows financial planners to simulate the impact of different budget allocation strategies before they are officially implemented. It allows for "what-if" testing of new risk-adjustment or population-based funding formulas. The intent is to identify the most effective and equitable way to distribute health funds while minimizing unintended consequences. It provides a safe environment for testing policy changes and optimizing the distribution of limited resources. Example: A simulation showing how changing a regional funding formula would impact the budget of small rural clinics versus large hospitals.
