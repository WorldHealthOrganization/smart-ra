Health Record Management Service ()
This service provides the core functionality for creating, updating, and querying longitudinal patient records. It supports the PHR apps, clinical portals, and third-party APIs that interact with health data. The purpose is to provide a standardized way to manage clinical data throughout its lifecycle. It ensures that health information is stored in a structured format that supports both care and analytics. Example: A FHIR-based service that allows a mobile app to retrieve a list of a patient’s active medications.
