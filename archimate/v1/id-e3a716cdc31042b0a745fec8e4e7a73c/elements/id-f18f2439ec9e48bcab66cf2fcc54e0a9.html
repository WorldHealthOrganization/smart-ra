Integrated Shared Care Plan (SCP) ()
Integrated Shared Care Plan (SCP), the structured, packaged output that consolidates the results of a comprehensive needs assessment into a coherent, deliverable artifact offered to providers and patients. This includes problems, goals, risks, care requirements, and patient preferences.
