Revenue performance analytics ()
This capability uses data processing and visualization tools to monitor the efficiency and reach of health revenue collection efforts. It analyzes trends in compliance, coverage, and financial leakage to provide a real-time view of fund inflows. The purpose is to provide decision-makers with the insights needed to improve collection strategies and address systemic weaknesses. It ensures that the revenue raising process is transparent and that collection targets are being met effectively. Example: A dashboard showing a 10% increase in tax compliance across the northern region after a new digital reminder system.
