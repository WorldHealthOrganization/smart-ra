Forecasting & Supply Planning System ()
Capture and analyze data on demand, inventory levels, open purchase orders, and pending replenishments to forecast and plan future needs, across programs and levels of care. Demand Planning involves forecasting future requirements based on historical patterns and anticipated future conditions. Supply Planning applies constraints including funding availability, procurement mechanisms, stock-on-hand, and lead times to develop executable plans that meet those needs.
