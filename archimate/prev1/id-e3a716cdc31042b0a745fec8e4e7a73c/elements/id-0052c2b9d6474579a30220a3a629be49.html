Audit & Compliance Service ()
This application service records healthcare activities, quality processes, and regulatory compliance information for monitoring and governance purposes. It supports audits, inspections, and verification of adherence to healthcare standards and policies. The purpose is to strengthen accountability, regulatory compliance, and healthcare governance. It also supports accreditation and quality assurance processes. Example: Healthcare auditors review digital records to verify compliance with national patient safety standards.
