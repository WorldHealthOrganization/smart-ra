Health Skills Gap and Training Analytics ()
The analytical capability to continuously process aggregate data regarding health worker skills, course enrollments, and professional certifications to isolate specific performance deficiencies and training shortages. The purpose of this capability is to guide the creation of targeted educational programs and professional development tracks by pinpointing exactly where the active workforce lacks the critical competencies required to meet patient care standards. Example: "The implementation of Health Skills Gap and Training Analytics highlighted an unexpectedly low completion rate for advanced digital triaging courses among urban clinic staff, leading to the deployment of targeted weekend training bootcamps."
