Patient & Family Empowerment Suite ()
A patient and family empowerment suite combines health information access, shared decision-making tools, education resources, and communication channels into a unified digital platform for patients and caregivers. The purpose of this suite is to give patients and families the information, tools, and channels they need to actively participate in care decisions and self-management. Example: A patient recovering from surgery uses the suite to view their discharge instructions, complete a daily symptom check-in, message their nurse with questions, and access educational videos about wound care. Their spouse is granted caregiver access to track recovery milestones.
