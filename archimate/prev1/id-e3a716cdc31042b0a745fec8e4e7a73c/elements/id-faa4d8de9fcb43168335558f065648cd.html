Differentiated/stratified contribution structures implemented and repeatable. ()
This outcome involves the implementation of tiered payment models that adjust health contributions based on economic status or demographic categories. It creates a repeatable logic for calculating premiums or taxes across diverse population segments. The intent is to ensure financial equity by making health coverage affordable for lower-income groups while maintaining fund sustainability. It allows the system to scale predictably as new population groups are integrated into the health scheme. Example: A digital system that automatically calculates reduced insurance premiums for informal workers based on verified income data.
