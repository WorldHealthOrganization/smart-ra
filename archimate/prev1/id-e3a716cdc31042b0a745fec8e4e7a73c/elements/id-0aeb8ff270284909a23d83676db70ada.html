Lifelong Health Record ()
A Shared Health Record is a national or federated platform that aggregates clinical data from various providers into a single longitudinal view. It functions as a central repository or a decentralized network linked via health information exchange standards. The purpose is to provide a complete medical history at the point of care to reduce clinical errors and administrative fragmentation. It ensures that health data follows the patient across different facilities and jurisdictions. Example: A centralized national database where a patient’s lab results from a private clinic and prescriptions from a public hospital are stored together.
