Provider payment schedules generated on time (linked to releases) ()
A critical operational milestone achieved by automatically calculating and finalizing healthcare provider disbursements according to strict, pre-established timelines. This automated scheduling directly links payment generation to the official release of public financial management funds, ensuring that administrative processing occurs in lockstep with actual cash availability. Its primary purpose is to eliminate chronic payment delays that threaten the financial stability of frontline healthcare facilities. By establishing a predictable and synchronized cash flow, it allows hospital administrators to confidently manage their operational budgets, maintain payroll, and prevent disruptions to patient care. Example: By linking the ministry of finance's monthly fund release directly to the health insurance database, the system automatically generates finalized payment schedules for all regional clinics within 24 hours of cash availability, ensuring doctors receive their salaries and medical suppliers are paid without administrative lag.
