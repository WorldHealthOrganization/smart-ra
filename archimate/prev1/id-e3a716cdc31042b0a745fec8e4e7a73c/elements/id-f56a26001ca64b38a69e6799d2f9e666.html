Decision Support Service ()
A decision support service evaluates patient information against clinical rules and evidence-based guidelines. It generates patient-specific recommendations, reminders, and alerts for healthcare providers. The purpose of this application service is to support healthcare providers in making accurate, timely, and evidence-based clinical decisions. It also improves care quality and reduces the likelihood of clinical errors and omissions. Example: A physician receives an automated alert recommending additional laboratory tests based on abnormal patient results entered into the electronic medical record system.
