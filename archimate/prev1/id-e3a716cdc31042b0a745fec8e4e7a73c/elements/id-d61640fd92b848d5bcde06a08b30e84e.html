Forecasting & scenario modelling engine ()
A specialized core application component that runs predictive statistical algorithms, machine learning models, and complex financial simulations to generate long-term health expenditure trends and evaluate the impact of hypothetical policy shifts. Its primary purpose is to serve as the system’s predictive sandbox, allowing decision-makers to stress-test financial choices, anticipate future cost spikes, and visually compare the outcomes of different funding strategies before codifying them into law. Example: The forecasting & scenario modeling engine processes ten years of historical claims data against projected population aging trends; when a planner runs a simulation for "Expanding free prescription coverage to citizens over 60," the engine calculates the future drug utilization spike and flags that the policy will trigger a $22 million annual deficit by year four unless the base premium is raised by 1.5%.
