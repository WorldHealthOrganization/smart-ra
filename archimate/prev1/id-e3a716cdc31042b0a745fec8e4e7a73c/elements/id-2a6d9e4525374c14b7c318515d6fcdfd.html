Detect and assess public health threats early ()
Detect and Assess Public Health Threats Early is a primary strategic goal centered on identifying biological, environmental, or chemical hazards at the absolute onset of their emergence. Its primary purpose is to preemptively analyze and evaluate the severity of a health risk before it can spread, giving health authorities a critical head start to mobilize defenses and save lives. Example: By continuously screening global air travel patterns and localized symptom spikes, epidemiologists can flag a novel respiratory virus at a single airport, allowing them to assess its transmission rate and issue containment protocols before it achieves community spread.
