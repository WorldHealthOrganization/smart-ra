Implement task-sharing and digital decision support systems to improve the continuity and quality of patient care across healthcare facilities over time to achieve integrated, person-centred care. ()
The strategic objective focused on distributing clinical tasks among multidisciplinary health teams and integrating point-of-care digital guidelines to deliver seamless, longitudinal, and holistic patient care. The purpose of this goal is to optimize the utilization of the available healthcare workforce, reduce clinical errors, and ensure that individuals receive consistent, high-quality treatment regardless of which facility or practitioner they visit over time. Example: "The successful execution of the 'Integrated, Person-Centred Care' goal allowed community health workers to utilize mobile digital decision support apps to safely manage and track stable hypertension patients, freeing up specialized physicians to handle complex cases."
