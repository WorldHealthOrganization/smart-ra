Fraud detection & prevention ()
This capability focuses on the proactive measures used to stop fraudulent financial activity before it results in a loss of funds. it includes real-time blocks on suspicious transactions and the continuous improvement of risk-scoring models. The purpose is to actively safeguard the financial health of the sector by reducing fraud losses and administrative errors. It creates a deterrent effect that discourages systemic abuse of health insurance and public funds. Example: A real-time block on a claim where the patient's ID is being used simultaneously at two different facilities 500 miles apart.
