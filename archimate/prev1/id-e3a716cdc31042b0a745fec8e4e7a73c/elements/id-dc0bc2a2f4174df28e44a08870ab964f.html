Continuous Growth by enabling capacity building and fostering career development ()
The targeted, strategic objective focused on creating structured, scalable frameworks that support the ongoing education, professional upskilling, and upward mobility of the healthcare workforce. The purpose of this goal is to improve health worker retention, boost morale, and ensure the entire health system dynamically adapts to evolving medical technologies and public health challenges through lifelong learning. Example: "The successful achievement of the 'Continuous Growth' goal through a state-funded initiative that provided rural midwives with digital pathways to earn advanced nursing certifications while maintaining their local practices."
