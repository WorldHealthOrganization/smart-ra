Measurable equity and inclusion improvements in decision-making. ()
This outcome reflects the documented reduction in disparities in patient participation, access to information, and influence over care decisions across demographic, geographic, and linguistic groups. The purpose of this outcome is to ensure that the benefits of patient and family engagement are not limited to digitally literate or well-resourced populations, but are equitably accessible to all patients regardless of background or circumstance. Example: A national health authority analyzes engagement platform data and finds that indigenous communities in rural areas have significantly lower care plan access rates than urban populations. This finding drives a targeted rollout of offline-capable mobile tools and community health worker-assisted onboarding in those regions.
