Faster detection and response to security incidents. ()
This represents a decrease in the time it takes to identify and neutralize cyber threats or data breaches within the health financial infrastructure. It relies on real-time security monitoring and pre-defined, automated incident response plans. It minimizes the damage caused by cyberattacks and protects the continuity of national financial and clinical operations. Rapid response is critical for maintaining the privacy of sensitive financial data and the continuous availability of the payment network. Example: A security system detecting and blocking a ransomware attack on the national claims database within seconds of its initiation.
