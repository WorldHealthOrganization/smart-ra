Pooling of funds: Manage pooled funds at scheme level for financial protection, coverage continuity, efficient resource use. ()
Pooling of funds is the management of collected health revenues at a scheme level to spread financial risks across the entire population. It minimizes the impact of high-cost medical events on any single individual by aggregating resources into a collective fund. Its purpose is to provide financial protection and ensure coverage continuity regardless of a person's individual health status or wealth. This mechanism promotes efficient resource use and maintains the stability of the health financing ecosystem through risk-sharing. Example: A national social health insurance fund that aggregates premiums from healthy workers to cover the medical costs of the elderly and chronically ill.
