Periodic olling budget forecasts produced on schedule ()
A continuous financial forecasting outcome achieved by regularly updating future spending projections at set intervals based on real-time public health expenditures and shifting operational needs. Its primary purpose is to provide financial decision-makers with an agile, forward-looking view of the budget, allowing them to adjust funding strategies mid-year and prevent unexpected deficits or unspent surpluses. Example: Every quarter, a public health agency pulls the latest expenditure data from its regional offices to automatically project spending for the next twelve months, allowing managers to reallocate excess administrative funds toward an unexpected outbreak response before the fiscal year ends.
