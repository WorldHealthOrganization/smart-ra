Longitudinal Patient Record & Care Pathway: A single, integrated care coordination timeline covering preventive, acute, chronic, and rehabilitative care. Patient- and provider-facing long-term care plans with milestones, goals, and monitoring indicators. Improved continuity of care metrics (e.g., fewer dropouts, adherence to chronic care guidelines). System-level outputs for population health analytics: care continuity index, chronic disease control rates, preventive care uptake.
