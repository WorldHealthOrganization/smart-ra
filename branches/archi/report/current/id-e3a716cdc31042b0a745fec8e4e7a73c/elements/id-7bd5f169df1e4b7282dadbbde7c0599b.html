Alert & Improvement Tracking Service ()
This application service identifies quality issues, performance deviations, and patient safety risks through automated monitoring and notification mechanisms. It tracks improvement actions, corrective measures, and follow-up activities over time. The purpose is to enable timely intervention and supports structured quality improvement management. It also helps organizations monitor the effectiveness of corrective actions and improvement initiatives. Example: A hospital quality system generates alerts when infection rates exceed acceptable thresholds and tracks remediation plans.
