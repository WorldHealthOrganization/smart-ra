Training Activity Tracking Service ()
The self-contained software component within the enterprise application layer that records, monitors, and audits individual health worker interactions, progress percentages, and milestones within accredited educational courses and professional development modules. The purpose of this service is to capture real-time learning data and participation logs, providing the secure operational evidence needed to feed downstream credentialing systems and compliance engines. Example: "The Training Activity Tracking Service logged every time a resident completed a module in the interactive radiology simulator, ensuring their hours were accurately captured for board review."
