Secure Messaging & Telehealth Service ()
This service enables secure, direct communication between patients, families, and care team members through messaging, voice, and video channels. It supports both asynchronous messaging and real-time virtual consultations within a governed, patient-safe environment. The purpose of this service is to extend access to care and provider communication beyond the physical clinic, reducing barriers related to distance, mobility, and availability. Example: A patient in a remote area uses the telehealth service to attend a follow-up consultation with their physician via video call, avoiding a four-hour journey to the nearest facility.
