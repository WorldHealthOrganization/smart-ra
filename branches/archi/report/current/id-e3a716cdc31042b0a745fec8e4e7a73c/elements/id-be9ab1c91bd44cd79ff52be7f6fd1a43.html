Increased accuracy and trust in surveillance data. ()
Increased Accuracy in Surveillance Data is a measurable public health outcome achieved by replacing manual reporting errors and data silos with automated, standardized data validation systems. Its primary purpose is to ensure that epidemiological decisions and resource allocations are based on flawless, high-quality information rather than flawed or incomplete estimates. For example, by integrating automated lab reporting that catches missing patient demographics or mismatched diagnostic codes, a health department can precisely track the exact demographics of a localized outbreak without relying on guesswork or delayed follow-up calls.
