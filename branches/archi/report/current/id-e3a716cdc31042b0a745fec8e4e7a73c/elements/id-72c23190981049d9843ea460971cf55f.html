Health Workers ()
An individual or group within the healthcare ecosystem who performs clinical and administrative duties and is directly impacted by how operational systems handle their compensation. They ensure patient care is delivered effectively while requiring transparent, dependable, and timely payroll operations to sustain their livelihood and focus on clinical outcomes. Example: "Registered Nurses, resident physicians, and medical laboratory technicians who rely on the hospital's digital payroll system to accurately reflect their night-shift differentials and overtime."
