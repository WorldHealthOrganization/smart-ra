Interoperability & Health Information Exchange ()
This capability involves the lifecycle management of longitudinal patient records, ensuring they are verifiable, accurate, and accessible over time. It encompasses the capture, storage, and retrieval of clinical data in a standardized format. The purpose is to maintain a continuous and trustworthy medical history for every individual throughout their life. It provides the foundational data layer required for integrated care and patient empowerment. Example: A system capability to link a patient’s childhood immunization record with their current health status 20 years later.
