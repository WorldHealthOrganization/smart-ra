Analytics & visualisation platform ()
This component provides the dashboards and reporting tools used to turn complex health financing data into visual charts and maps. It allows stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) through easy-to-understand visual interfaces. Its purpose is to drive transparency and data-driven decision-making across all levels of the health system. It ensures that policymakers, managers, and the public can easily see how health funds are being utilized and where performance gaps exist. Example: A public web dashboard that visualizes hospital expenditure and service quality across every district in the country.
