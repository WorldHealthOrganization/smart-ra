High Level View - Health Financing
Public Financial Management Health sector monitoring and auditing: Ensure accountable use of health funds through auditability, oversight reporting and appropriate public disclosure
Public Financial Management Pooling of funds: Allocate pooled health funds equitably across programmes, regions and populations to support redistributive capacity and to achieve financial protection
Public Financial Management Budget planning, execution and oversight (monitoring): Ensure health budgets are planned, executed and monitored to support efficient budget execution, transparency and providers paid on time.
Public Financial Management Revenue raising (e.g., taxes, insurance contributions): Secure sufficient and sustainable funds for the health sector in an efficient and equitable way
Ensure effective, equitable and sustainable health financing and public financial management receive reliable digital tools to support intermediate UHC objectives (equitable distribution of resources, efficiency, transparency) and final UHC goals (access in line with need, financial protection, quality of care) Public Financial Management
