Interoperability Gateway ()
This outcome is the clinical benefit of interoperability, where providers have access to a patient’s full history across different sites. It leads to more efficient care pathways and the elimination of redundant medical activities. The purpose is to enhance the quality of patient care while reducing health system waste and costs. By having the right data at the right time, providers can avoid duplicate tests and make safer clinical decisions. Example: A 20% reduction in repeat laboratory tests due to the availability of recent results in the shared health record.
