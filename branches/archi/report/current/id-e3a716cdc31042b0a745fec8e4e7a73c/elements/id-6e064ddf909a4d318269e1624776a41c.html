Event signing and trusted timestamping ()
The self-contained software component within the enterprise application layer that applies cryptographic signatures and cryptographically secure time-markers to transactional logs, credential changes, and system activities. The purpose of this service is to establish non-repudiation and proof of existence for critical operational data, ensuring that an event's data integrity and the exact moment it occurred cannot be retroactively altered, falsified, or contested. Example: "The Event Signing and Trusted Timestamping service automatically sealed a surgeon's digital signature on a blood transfusion authorization, creating a permanent, tamper-proof record that legally verified exactly when the procedure was approved."
