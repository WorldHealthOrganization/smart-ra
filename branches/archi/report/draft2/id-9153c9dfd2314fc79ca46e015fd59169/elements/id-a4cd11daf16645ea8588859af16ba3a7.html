Value ()
The benefit or utility gained by a stakeholder from using a service, product, or capability. Value captures why a goal or capability matters and who benefits. Value can be monetary, but in health contexts it often reflects non-monetary benefits such as trust, usability, or equity. For example, a citizen values secure access to their data, while a health worker values having a complete clinical history at the point of care.
