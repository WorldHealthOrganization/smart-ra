Data warehouse ()
A data warehouse is a large, centralized repository that stores historical data aggregated from multiple clinical and financial sources. It is designed for complex querying and analysis rather than day-to-day transactional processing. Its purpose is to provide a "single source of truth" for long-term health sector analytics and reporting. By consolidating disparate data, it allows for deep analysis of spending trends, service utilization, and epidemiological shifts over time. Example: A centralized server that stores ten years of patient claims data and hospital expenditure records for national research.
