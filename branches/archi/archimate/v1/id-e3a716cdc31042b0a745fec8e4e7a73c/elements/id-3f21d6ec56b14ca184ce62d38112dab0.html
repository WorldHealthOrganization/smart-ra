Secure Messaging Service ()
This service provides encrypted, role-based messaging between care team members within a clinical environment. It ensures that patient-related communications are delivered securely and linked to the appropriate clinical context. The purpose of this service is to replace informal or unsecured communication channels with a reliable, auditable messaging infrastructure. It also ensures that sensitive patient information is protected throughout all exchanges. Example: A nurse sends a secure message to an on-call physician regarding a change in a patient's condition, with the message automatically tagged to the patient's active encounter.
