Finance ()
These are the administrative staff within government and insurance agencies who process the daily transactions, claims, and reports of the health financing system. They are the primary users of the operational tools for health finance. The purpose is to reduce their manual workload and improve the speed and accuracy of financial processing through automation. They value tools that simplify complex workflows and provide proactive alerts for issues. Example: A claims officer using an automated rules engine to process 500 routine claims in a single hour without manual intervention.
