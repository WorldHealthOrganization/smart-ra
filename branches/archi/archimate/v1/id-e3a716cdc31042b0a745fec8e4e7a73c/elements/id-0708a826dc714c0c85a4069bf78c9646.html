Medium-term expenditure projections produced. ()
A strategic planning outcome where the government generates multi-year financial forecasts that map out expected healthcare costs, revenue trends, and funding requirements over a rolling three-to-five-year horizon. Its primary purpose is to move away from reactive, year-to-year budgeting by providing policymakers with a clear forward-looking roadmap to ensure that long-term healthcare commitments—like building hospitals or expanding universal coverage—remain financially stable and sustainable. Example: Achieving this medium-term expenditure projections outcome allows the health ministry to present a five-year fiscal forecast to parliament, demonstrating that while the rollout of a national subsidized diabetes program will increase spending by 7% annually, it will be fully offset by projected sin-tax revenues, preventing a future deficit.
