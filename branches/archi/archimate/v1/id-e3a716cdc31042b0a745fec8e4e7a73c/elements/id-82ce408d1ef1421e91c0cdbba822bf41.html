Reduced inequities in per-capita allocations across regions/programmes. ()
This outcome represents a narrowing of the gap in health spending per person between different provinces or health initiatives. It signifies that funding is becoming more balanced and is increasingly aligned with specific regional needs rather than historical trends. The objective is to ensure that a citizen’s access to quality care is not determined solely by their geographic location. It drives toward universal health coverage by ensuring a minimum standard of per-capita investment across the entire nation. Example: Closing the 50% funding gap that previously existed between the capital city and remote mountainous regions over a three-year period.
