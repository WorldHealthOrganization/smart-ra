Strategic Purchasing & Provider Payment ()
This value stream manages the active procurement of healthcare services from providers using evidence-based contracts and standardized payment models. It transitions health financing from passive budget transfers to result-oriented reimbursement cycles. The goal is to maximize the value of health spending by incentivizing high-quality care and improving clinical efficiency. It ensures that healthcare providers are paid fairly and promptly according to established performance metrics and tariffs. Example: A social health fund reimbursing hospitals based on "cost-per-case" bundles rather than fixed annual grants.
