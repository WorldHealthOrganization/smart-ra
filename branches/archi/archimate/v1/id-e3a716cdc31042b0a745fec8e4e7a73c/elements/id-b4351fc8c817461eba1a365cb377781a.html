Terminology & Data Standards Management ()
This capability provides the technical means to uniquely identify citizens and verify their identity before allowing access to records. It often links a health-specific ID to a national registry or digital identity provider. The purpose is to ensure that health records are linked to the correct person and that unauthorized access is prevented. This is a critical security layer for protecting patient privacy and ensuring data integrity. Example: A biometric-based login service that authenticates a patient before displaying their medical results.
