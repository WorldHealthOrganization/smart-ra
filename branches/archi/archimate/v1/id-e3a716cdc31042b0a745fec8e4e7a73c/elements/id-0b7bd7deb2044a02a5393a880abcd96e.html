Consent & Preference Management Service ()
This service manages the digital capture, storage, and enforcement of patient consents and care preferences across the health platform. It ensures that access to patient information and participation in care activities is always governed by the patient's recorded wishes. The purpose of this service is to uphold patient autonomy and legal compliance by ensuring that consent is explicit, informed, and consistently applied across all systems and interactions. Example: A patient updates their consent preferences to restrict access to their mental health records from non-specialist providers. The service automatically enforces these restrictions across all connected systems and logs the change for audit purposes.
