Shared Health Record ()
The Shared Health Record is a clinical data repository that stores a person’s longitudinal health information in a standardized, interoperable format. It aggregates data from various points of care, such as hospitals, clinics, and labs, to provide a comprehensive view of a patient’s medical history. Its purpose is to ensure that healthcare providers have timely access to a patient’s full medical history at the point of care. This improves clinical decision-making, enhances patient safety, and supports continuity of care across different facilities. Example: A centralized platform where a primary care doctor can view laboratory results and discharge summaries from a patient's recent specialist visit.
