Longitudinal Care Coordination Management ()
Longitudinal Care Coordination Management: Create and maintain continuous care pathways for chronic, preventive, and complex conditions. Support multi-disciplinary care teams with defined roles over time. Enable task-sharing and delegation (e.g., between physicians, nurses, community workers). Integrate decision support for guideline-based long-term management. Maintain a longitudinal health record linked to patient identity (MPI). Facilitate alerts for care gaps, preventive screenings, and follow-ups.
