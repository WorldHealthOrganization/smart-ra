Integrated Shared Care Plan (SCP) ()
Integrated Shared Care Plan (SCP): A dynamic, longitudinal care plan accessible and updatable by all authorized providers. Includes problems, goals, interventions, responsibilities, progress, and patient preferences. Patient-facing view with personalized instructions and self-management support. Population analytics product → aggregated data for care coordination KPIs (e.g., readmission reduction, continuity index).
