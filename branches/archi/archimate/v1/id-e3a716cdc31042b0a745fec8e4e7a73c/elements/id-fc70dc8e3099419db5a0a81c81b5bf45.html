Quality Analytics Engine ()
This application component processes healthcare quality and operational data to generate insights, predictive analyses, trends, and performance evaluations. It supports advanced analytics for healthcare quality management and decision-making. The purpose is to enable evidence-based quality improvement and supports proactive identification of healthcare risks and performance gaps. It also enhances reporting and strategic healthcare planning. Example: The analytics engine identifies hospitals with increasing readmission trends and predicts facilities at risk of performance decline.
