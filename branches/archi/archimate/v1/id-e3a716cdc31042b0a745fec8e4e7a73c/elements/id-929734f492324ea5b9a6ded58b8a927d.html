Streamline healthcare financing (Operational efficiency) ()
This sub-goal aims to reduce the manual workload and administrative delays in the health financing lifecycle through process automation and workflow orchestration. It targets faster cycle times for provider payments and improved budget execution. The purpose is to enhance the operational efficiency of health finance teams and improve the cash flow of healthcare providers. This leads to a more responsive health system where resources move quickly to where they are needed. Example: Implementing robotic process automation (RPA) to handle the initial intake and validation of thousands of monthly medical claims.
Streamline healthcare financing (Operational efficiency) Utilize digital solutions to streamline healthcare financing and strategic payment processes for improved efficiency and transparency.
