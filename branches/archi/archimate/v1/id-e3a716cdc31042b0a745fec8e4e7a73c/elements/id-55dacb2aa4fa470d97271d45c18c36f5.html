Enrolment & coverage scheme discovery ()
This capability enables individuals to find and register for the health insurance or social protection schemes they are eligible for. It provides the tools for citizens to navigate available coverage options and complete the necessary registration steps. The goal is to maximize population coverage by reducing the barriers to entering the health insurance system. It ensures that citizens are aware of their financial protection options and can easily secure their right to health benefits. Example: A mobile portal where a citizen enters their national ID to see which government-subsidized health plans they can join.
