Data aggregation and consolidation ()
This service collects clinical and financial data from disparate healthcare facilities and organizes it into a centralized system. It normalizes varying data formats to provide a single, unified view of sector-wide health spending. The objective is to eliminate data silos and provide a consistent foundation for national health reporting. It ensures that decision-makers have access to a comprehensive and accurate dataset for high-level financial oversight. Example: A service that merges monthly expenditure reports from 50 regional hospitals into a single national health ledger.
