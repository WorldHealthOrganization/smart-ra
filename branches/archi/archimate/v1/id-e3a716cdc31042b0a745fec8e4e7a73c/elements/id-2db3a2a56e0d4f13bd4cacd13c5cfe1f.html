Learning & Development Planning Service ()
The self-contained software component within the enterprise application layer that enables healthcare managers and individual practitioners to design, schedule, and track personalized multi-year educational pathways and institutional upskilling roadmaps. The purpose of this service is to align long-term workforce capability development with systemic healthcare goals by projecting future skill requirements and automatically recommending targeted training trajectories for health workers. Example: "The Learning & Development Planning Service analyzed a hospital's upcoming transition to electronic health records and automatically built a six-month training roadmap for the entire nursing staff to ensure smooth adoption."
