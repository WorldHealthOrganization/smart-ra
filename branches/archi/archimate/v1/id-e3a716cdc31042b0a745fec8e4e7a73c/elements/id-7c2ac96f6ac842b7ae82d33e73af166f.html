Payment adapter / reconciliation service ()
Sits between the core health finance system and external banking networks to translate, route, and automatically match incoming digital transactions against open invoices. Its primary purpose is to automate the labor-intensive process of clearing payments, ensuring that bank transfers, mobile money deposits, and card transactions are instantly recognized, verified, and closed out on the master ledger without human intervention. Example: When an employer pays their monthly health insurance premium via an external commercial bank portal, the payment adapter / reconciliation service ingests the raw bank transaction file, translates its proprietary layout into the health system's standard format, and matches the cash directly to the employer's account balance within seconds.
