Improved adherence to treatments and care transitions. ()
This outcome ensures that patients follow through on prescribed treatments, lifestyle recommendations, and care transition instructions. It reduces the drop-off that commonly occurs when patients leave a clinical setting without adequate follow-up mechanisms. The purpose of this outcome is to improve health outcomes by addressing one of the most significant barriers to effective care. It also reduces avoidable readmissions and complications by keeping patients informed and supported between encounters. Example: A patient discharged after a cardiac event receives automated daily medication reminders, a structured recovery checklist, and a scheduled check-in message from their care team through the engagement platform. Their adherence data is tracked and shared with the cardiologist ahead of the follow-up visit.
