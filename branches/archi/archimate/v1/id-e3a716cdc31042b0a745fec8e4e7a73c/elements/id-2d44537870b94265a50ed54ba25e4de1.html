Health Mgmt Info Service ()
A health management information service collects, manages, and analyzes healthcare operational and clinical data. It supports reporting, monitoring, and healthcare program management activities. The purpose of this application component is to enable evidence-based planning and decision-making within healthcare organizations and government agencies. It also provides reliable information for monitoring health outcomes and system performance. Example: A national dashboard monitors immunization coverage and disease incidence across multiple regions.
