Digital signing and verification ()
This service provides the technical tools to apply and check cryptographic signatures on clinical and financial documents. It ensures that the source of a document can be proven and that it has not been modified since it was signed. The goal is to provide non-repudiable trust for all digital transactions within the health network. It ensures that clinical evidence and payment authorizations are legally binding and completely tamper-proof. Example: A service that checks the digital signature on a lab report before it is used as evidence to authorize a provider's claim.
