Improved collection performance (coverage, compliance, reduced leakage) reported routinely. ()
A high-efficiency governance outcome where automated tracking systems consistently expand the tax base, catch non-payers, and stop financial waste, with the progress documented in regular public reports. Its primary purpose is to prove the revenue system is working efficiently by demonstrating that more people are paying their fair share and that money is no longer disappearing into bureaucratic cracks. Example: After connecting its registration database with the corporate tax network, the health ministry automatically flags businesses hiding payroll data, boosting contribution collections by 14% and generating a monthly performance report that proves exactly how much leakage was stopped.
