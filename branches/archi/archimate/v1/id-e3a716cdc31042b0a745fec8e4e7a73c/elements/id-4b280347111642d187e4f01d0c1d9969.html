Risk equalisation / allocation engine ()
This engine is a specialized mathematical tool that calculates fund transfers between different insurance pools based on their members' health risks. It uses actuarial formulas to redistribute resources from lower-risk groups to those with sicker or older populations. Its purpose is to promote financial equity and prevent "cherry-picking" where insurers only target healthy people. It ensures that all health funds remain solvent and capable of providing care regardless of the risk profile of their specific members. E xample: A calculation engine that determines how much money an urban health fund must transfer to a rural fund with high chronic disease rates.
