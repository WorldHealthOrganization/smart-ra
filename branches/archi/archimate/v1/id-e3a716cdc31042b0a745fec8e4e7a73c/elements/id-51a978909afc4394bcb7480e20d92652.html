Payment flow monitoring and reconciliation ()
An advanced application service that automatically tracks financial transfers from the moment a taxpayer initiates a transaction until the money settles in the national health fund, continuously cross-referencing bank records against internal ledger invoices to ensure perfect alignment. Its primary purpose is to eliminate manual bookkeeping bottlenecks and prevent financial discrepancies by instantly highlighting un-reconciled deposits, failed bank transfers, or accounting errors. Example: The payment flow monitoring and reconciliation service reviews thousands of digital health insurance deposits every midnight, instantly matching 99.8% of the cash drops to specific employer invoices and flagging the remaining handful of unmatched bank transfers for immediate human review.
