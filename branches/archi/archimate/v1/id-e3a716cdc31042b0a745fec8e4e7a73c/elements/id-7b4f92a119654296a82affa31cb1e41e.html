Establish a secure, universally accessible, verifiable health record for every individual ()
This sub-goal aims to create a national infrastructure where every citizen has a digital health record that is secure and universally available. It focuses on issuing verifiable credentials that can be used across the entire care continuum. The purpose is to ensure life-long health data portability and continuity of care for all individuals. It addresses the "Right to Health" by providing an equitable and person-centric data ownership model. Example: A national mandate to issue a digital health identity and record to every newborn and existing citizen.
