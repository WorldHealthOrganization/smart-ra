High Level View - Health Financing ()
Ensure effective, equitable and sustainable health financing and public financial management receive reliable digital tools to support intermediate UHC objectives (equitable distribution of resources, efficiency, transparency) and final UHC goals (access in line with need, financial protection, quality of care)
Public Financial Management
Budget planning, execution and oversight (monitoring): Ensure efficient, transparent health budgets with timely provider payments.
Revenue raising for public financial management (e.g., taxes, insurance contributions): Secure sustainable, equitable health sector funds.
Pooling of funds: Allocate pooled funds equitably across programs, regions, populations.
Health sector monitoring and auditing: Ensure accountable health fund use through audits, oversight, disclosure.
Health financing to provide coverage
Revenue raising for health financing to provide coverage (e.g., taxes, insurance contributions): Secure sustainable, equitable health sector funds.
Targeting & identification: Ensure all individuals (across diverse population segments such as vulnerable, informal, and formal) are identified and correctly targeted for coverage and benefits, enabling affordable and equitable access and real-time eligibility decisions.
Pooling of funds: Manage pooled funds at scheme level for financial protection, coverage continuity, efficient resource use.
Purchasing (including contracting, provider payments, claims management, benefits specification): Ensure affordable, efficient purchasing with timely payments, cost control, transparency.
Benefits design: Define coherent entitlements, access conditions, services, populations, price/non-price terms.
Safeguarding: Enforce privacy, security, fraud controls across coverage operations.
