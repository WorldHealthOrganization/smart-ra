Health Facility Logistics Management System ()
The capabilities necessary to managing the flow of stock into the health facility, its current disposition, and the flow of stock out of the facility. In this case, outbound processing describes when a facility is also acting as a local redistribution hub that is able to share or move stock to other facilities.
