Scheme fund balance and utilisation reports. ()
These are routine financial statements that show the current status of a health insurance pool, including total income, total spending, and the remaining balance. They provide a high-level view of the fund's fiscal trajectory and overall health. They allow managers to monitor the solvency of health schemes and make informed decisions about benefit packages or required premium adjustments. Regular reporting ensures that all stakeholders are aware of the fund's ability to meet its future obligations. Example: A quarterly dashboard showing that the Social Health Insurance fund has utilized 45% of its annual budget by the mid-year mark.
