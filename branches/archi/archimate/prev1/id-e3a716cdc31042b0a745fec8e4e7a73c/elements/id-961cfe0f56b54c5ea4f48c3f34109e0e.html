DPI-H National Health Workforce Directory/Registry ()
The self-contained software component within the national digital health infrastructure that serves as the single, authoritative source of truth for authenticating health worker identities, credentials, and professional statuses. It maintains a secure, real-time database of unique practitioner identifiers, active licenses, and current employment deployments across both public and private sectors. It eliminates fraud, credential misrepresentation, and data silos by providing a standardized, centralized interface for verifying a clinician's right to practice. This trustworthy verification ensures that payroll systems can automatically cross-reference active license statuses before authorizing professional wage tiers and role-based shift differentials. Example: "The National Health Workforce Registry module that validates a physician's active medical license and unique practitioner ID before the hospital's payroll system approves their specialized clinical salary rate."
