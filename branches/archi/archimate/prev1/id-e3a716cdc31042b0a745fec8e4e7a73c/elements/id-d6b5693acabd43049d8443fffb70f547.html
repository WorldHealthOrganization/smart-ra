Community Health Info System ()
This application component captures and manages health-related data from community-based healthcare services, outreach activities, and population health programs. It supports monitoring of community-level health indicators and interventions. The purpose is to strengthen population health management and improves visibility into community healthcare performance and outcomes. It also supports planning and evaluation of public health initiatives. Example: Community health workers record household nutrition and immunization data through a mobile health reporting application.
