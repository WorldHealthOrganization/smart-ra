Personal Health Records Creation ()
This value stream encompasses the initial registration of an individual and the setup of their foundational digital profile. It involves capturing baseline demographics and linking them to a unique, verifiable health identifier. The purpose is to provide every person with a trusted entry point into the digital health ecosystem. It establishes the necessary structure for building a lifelong, longitudinal record that follows the patient across all healthcare facilities. Example: A community health worker registering a citizen and issuing a digital health ID that links to the central Client Registry.
