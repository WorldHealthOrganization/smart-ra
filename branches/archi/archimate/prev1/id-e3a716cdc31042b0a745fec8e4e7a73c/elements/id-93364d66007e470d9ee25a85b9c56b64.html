Contact Tracing System ()
A contact tracing system in healthcare surveillance is a structured network of public health tools and protocols designed to systematically identify, assess, and monitor individuals who have been exposed to an infectious disease. Its primary purpose is to interrupt the chain of transmission by rapidly isolating confirmed cases and quarantining exposed contacts before they can spread the pathogen further into the community. Example: During a regional measles outbreak, health officials use the system to track down anyone who shared a clinic waiting room with an infected patient, advising them to monitor for symptoms or seek immediate vaccination
