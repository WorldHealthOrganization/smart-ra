Facility Registry ()
The Facility Registry metadata component maintains structured information about health facilities, such as ownership, location, operational status, and identifiers. It provides the metadata necessary to authenticate a facility’s role in the ecosystem. Its purpose is to supply the context needed for data provenance and verification of health services. It ensures that every clinical event is attributed to a specific, verified healthcare delivery site. Example: Metadata describing a facility as a "Level 3 General Hospital" with a specific geographic coordinate and license number.
