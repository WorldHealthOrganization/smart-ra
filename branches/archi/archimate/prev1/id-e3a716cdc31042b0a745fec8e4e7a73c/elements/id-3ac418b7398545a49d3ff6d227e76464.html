Increased provider confidence and patient trust in care quality ()
Clinical decision support tools provide healthcare providers with reliable and evidence-based recommendations during patient consultations. This strengthens transparency and consistency in healthcare delivery. The purpose of this outcome is to improve provider confidence in clinical decision-making and increases patient trust in treatment recommendations. It also encourages greater adherence to approved care standards and protocols. Example: Patients are more confident in treatment plans when clinicians reference nationally approved medical guidelines during consultations.
