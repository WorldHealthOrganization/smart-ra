Strengthen adverse event reporting (AER) and pharmacovigilance ()
Strengthen Adverse Event Reporting (AER) and Pharmacovigilance is a critical safety goal focused on optimizing the speed, compliance, and depth with which medical product complications are tracked and analyzed post-approval. Its primary purpose is to establish a hyper-vigilant safety net that rapidly uncovers hidden risks in drugs, vaccines, and medical devices, ensuring public trust and preventing widespread patient harm. Example: By mandating automated electronic health record triggers for suspected drug toxicities and utilizing machine learning to scour global safety databases, health authorities can instantly detect a rare cardiovascular risk in a newly released medication, allowing them to issue immediate black-box warnings or market restrictions.
