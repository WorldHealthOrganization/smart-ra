Identity confirmation at point of service ()
This service uses digital credentials or biometrics to verify the physical identity of a patient during a healthcare encounter. It ensures that the individual seeking care is the legitimate owner of the health identity being presented to the provider. The purpose is to prevent identity theft and the fraudulent use of health benefits by non-eligible individuals. It ensures that medical records are linked to the correct person, which is essential for patient safety and financial integrity. Example: A clinic using a fingerprint scan to authenticate a patient before verifying their insurance eligibility.
