PKI / CA + digital signature & timestamping service ()
An advanced technical component that provides the underlying cryptographic infrastructure required to secure digital identities and validate critical transactions across the healthcare network. This component combines a Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) and Certificate Authority (CA) to issue, manage, and revoke digital certificates, alongside a dedicated digital signature and timestamping engine that binds those certificates to specific financial and administrative events. Its primary purpose is to establish absolute cryptographic trust, authenticity, and legal non-repudiation for all health sector operations. By assigning unique, unforgeable digital identities to personnel and systems, it ensures that every approval, fund disbursement, or contract modification is sealed with a signature that proves exactly who authorized it, while an independent timestamp verifies precisely when it happened—rendering the record completely tamper-evident. Example: When a hospital administrator signs off on a bulk purchase of specialized medical hardware, the system's PKI/CA + digital signature and timestamping service processes the transaction; it validates the administrator's digital certificate against the internal CA, generates a unique cryptographic signature for the procurement document, and links it to an atomic time server, guaranteeing the contract cannot be altered, forged, or backdated.
