Analytics and Risk Assessment Service ()
An Analytics and Risk Assessment Service is a predictive public health intelligence platform that applies statistical models, machine learning, and epidemiological data to evaluate population health threats. Its primary purpose is to transform raw health data into actionable insights, allowing authorities to forecast disease trends, identify vulnerable demographics, and proactively allocate medical resources. Example: Analyzing real-time emergency room admissions, local weather patterns, and regional vaccination rates, the service can flag a high risk of an impending influenza surge in specific neighborhoods, prompting targeted public health warnings
