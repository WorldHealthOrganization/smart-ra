Data Reporting & Visualization Service ()
The specific application service that extracts raw data from various systems and transforms it into visual charts, graphs, and dashboards. It provides a standardized interface for users to generate reports and interact with operational metrics in real time. It allows non-technical administrators and managers to quickly interpret complex payroll and workforce trends without querying databases manually. This visual access speeds up decision-making and makes it easier to share performance insights across different hospital departments. Example: "The business intelligence reporting service that automatically generates monthly labor cost charts and displays overtime spikes on the HR management dashboard."
