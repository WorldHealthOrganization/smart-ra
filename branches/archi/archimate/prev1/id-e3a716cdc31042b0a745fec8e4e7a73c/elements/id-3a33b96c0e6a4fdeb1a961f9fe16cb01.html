Real-time eligibility and entitlement decisions at point of service. ()
This refers to the ability of the health system to provide instant verification of a patient's coverage and benefits at the point of care. It utilizes high-speed digital queries to check the current status of an individual within the insurance registry. The objective is to provide a seamless and dignified experience for patients while reducing administrative uncertainty for providers. It ensures that clinical services can begin without delay and that financial liability is understood immediately. Example: A hospital receptionist receiving an instant "Authorized" status and benefit summary upon scanning a patient’s digital health ID.
