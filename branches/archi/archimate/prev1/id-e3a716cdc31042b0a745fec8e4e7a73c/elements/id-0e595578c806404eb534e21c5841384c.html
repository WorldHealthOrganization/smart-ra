Integrated Clinical Decision Support Suite ()
An integrated clinical decision support suite combines multiple decision support tools into a unified healthcare platform. It provides alerts, reminders, recommendations, and predictive analytics within clinical workflows. The purpose of this suite is to enhance the efficiency, quality, and safety of clinical decision-making processes. It also ensures that providers receive timely and actionable insights during patient care. Example: An electronic medical record system displays medication safety alerts and preventive care reminders during patient consultations.
