National Health worker Qualifications and Credentials Catalogue ()
The master, authoritative data repository that serves as the single source of truth for all officially recognized health worker professions, educational requirements, clinical competencies, and licensing standards nationwide. This catalog maps out the structural taxonomy of the healthcare workforce, detailing the exact criteria, renewal cycles, and mandatory certifications required for every specialized role in the health system. Possessing this catalog eliminates institutional confusion, inconsistent hiring standards, and the fragmented naming of medical roles across public, private, and regional facilities. For regulatory boards and human resource planners, it provides a unified framework to systematically audit existing staff qualifications, align educational pipelines with national healthcare standards, and instantly verify that a practitioner's active credentials match their legal scope of practice. Example: "The implementation of the National Health Worker Qualifications and Credentials Catalogue, which standardized the registration codes for all specialized nurse practitioners, enabling the automated verification of their clinical scopes across all private and public hospitals."
