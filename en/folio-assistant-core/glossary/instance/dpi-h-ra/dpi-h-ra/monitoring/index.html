Monitoring candidate

The continuous process of collecting and analysing data to compare how well an intervention is being implemented against expected results (8).

Also called: monitoring

SFP handbook glossary wording, verbatim (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, page-010.md, p. 10, citing Monitoring and evaluating digital health interventions, WHO 2016). Wording alternative in DIIG Annex 1.1 Glossary (who-iris/library/9789240010567-eng/sections/page-141.md, p. 129, citing WHO evaluation practice handbook, 2013): "The process of collecting and analysing routinely collected data to compare how well an intervention is being implemented against expected results and measure changes in performance over time." Both definitions are recorded as candidates; neither is chosen (smart-ra#133 S1, dth-sfp#1 §2.1 Cluster B1).
