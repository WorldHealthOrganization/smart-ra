Health Workforce Registry (HWR) candidate

The Health Workforce Registry is the national authoritative record of identity, credential, and classification data for all health workers recognised as part of the national health workforce — including licensed or certified clinical professionals, community health workers, allied health personnel, and administrative and management staff in health-related roles.

Also called: HWR, Health Worker Registry

Verbatim lead sentence from Appendix C (Health Workforce Registry, 1 What It Is). Also called Health Worker Registry (HWR) in OpenHIE alignment notes and downstream Digital Transformation Handbooks. A candidate until the RA editors adopt it through the public review.
