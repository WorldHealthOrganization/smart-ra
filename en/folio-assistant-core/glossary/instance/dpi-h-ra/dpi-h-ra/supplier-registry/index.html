Supplier Registry (SR) candidate

The Supplier Registry is the authoritative master data source for identity, classification, and regulatory status of suppliers engaged in the health supply chain — including manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers, third-party logistics providers, importers, and procurement agents.

Also called: SR

Verbatim lead sentence from Appendix C (Supplier Registry, 1 What It Is). A candidate until the RA editors adopt it through the public review.
