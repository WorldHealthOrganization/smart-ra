Terminology Service (TS) candidate

The Terminology Service is a governed, publicly accessible service that manages, publishes, and provides access to the standardised terminologies, code systems, value sets, and concept maps used across the digital health ecosystem.

Also called: TS

Verbatim lead sentence from Appendix C (Terminology Service, 1 What It Is). Abbreviated TS throughout the RA draft and downstream Digital Transformation Handbooks. A candidate until the RA editors adopt it through the public review.
