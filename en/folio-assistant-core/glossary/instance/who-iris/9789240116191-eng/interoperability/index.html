Interoperability candidate

Ability of different applications to access, exchange, integrate and use data in a coordinated and consistent manner through the use of shared application interfaces, value sets, concepts and standards, within and across organizational, regional and national boundaries, to provide timely and seamless portability of information and optimize health outcomes.
