Section 3.6.4 contains the document's strongest normative trust statement: a cross-jurisdiction trust framework, with mutual recognition of identities, credentials and digital signatures underpinned by appropriate public-key infrastructure, shall establish that participants and the data they exchange can be trusted (lines 945-947). 'Appropriate' is undefined, and the GDHCN pattern cited immediately above verifies document provenance between national authorities; it does not authenticate live exchange participants, nor anything below authority level, such as a hospital verifying a foreign hospital or a clinician's role verified across a border.
