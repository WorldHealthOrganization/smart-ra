The LHR is described as the primary source for HMIS aggregates, which contradicts real RHIS architecture and also the first HMIS "core attribute". DHIS2 supports direct aggregate reporting, event data and registered Tracker data concurrently; it can analyse them together or transform Tracker indicators into aggregate values. This mixed-modality model preserves continuity while facilities and programmes transition at different speeds. Furthermore, HMIS covers a variety of inputs and outputs that are not based on shared patient records (CHW services, health facility assessments, health education services/outreach, stock availability, to name a few) Source: https://docs.dhis2.org/en/implement/maintenance-and-use/tracker-and-aggregate-data-integration.html
