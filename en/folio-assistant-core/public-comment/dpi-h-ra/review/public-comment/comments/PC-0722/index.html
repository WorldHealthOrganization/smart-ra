I was again checking the HF registry section and wanted to comment on the use of the term “CGR.” At WHO, we have generally avoided this terminology and instead use “geospatial registry,” which is a broader and more globally applicable concept. Many advanced countries may not readily adopt the CGR concept because they already have well-established and continuously updated addressing systems. In addition, geospatial registries are cross-cutting in nature and not specific to the health sector. For this reason, I would be cautious about introducing terminology that may not be fully endorsed or used across sectors beyond health. My suggestion is to replace references to CGR with “national geospatial registry” or simply “geospatial registry,” depending on the context in which the term is used.
