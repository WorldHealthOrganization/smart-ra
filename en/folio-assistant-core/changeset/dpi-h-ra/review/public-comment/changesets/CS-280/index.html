title
Support or qualify outcome and benefit claims
requirements
- Cite evidence or label claims as recognised risks or expected benefits - Qualify claimed benefits (adherence, transparency, reduced burden, faster detection) and name the non-digital conditions they depend on - Qualify or evidence generalisations presented as findings (entry-point trajectories, characteristic failure modes, Phase 1 returns, causes of implementation failure) - State that outcomes are not automatic and depend on quality and extent of implementation and context - Cite the source or use general wording - Replace absolute claims (complete history, all systems draw from one master) with measured wording - Where claims are retained, require measurement or evaluation in context - Present SGI's contribution as integration of existing practice, not a new discipline
