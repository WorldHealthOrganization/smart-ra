title
Define AI lifecycle governance and post-deployment controls
requirements
- Consolidate the safeguards already named in 5.4 into a testable minimum control checklist (or cross-reference one) - Describe a national AI lifecycle from need identification to monitoring and decommissioning - Require human accountability and contestability for resource, eligibility and care decisions - Describe governance of learned models used in decision support, surveillance and analytics without a central AI component - Note regulatory frameworks such as EU MDR that apply to clinical decision-support tools - Include named clinical ownership, versioning, logging, incident reporting and link to routine clinical governance - Extend the conformance model with AI-specific evidence: model identity, versioning, drift, human oversight - Add post-deployment monitoring, incident reporting, rollback and notification
