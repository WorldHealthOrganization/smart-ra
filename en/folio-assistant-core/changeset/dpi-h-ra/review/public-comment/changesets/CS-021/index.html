title
Align the health workforce goal with NHWA and a system perspective
requirements
- Add the health-system planning perspective and equitable distribution - Distinguish workforce and facility registries from HRIS, payroll, learning and planning systems the goal also needs - Align with NHWA distinctions (licensed, professionally active, practising) and the health labour market framework - Separate registration from competence, privileging and scope of practice; cover non-licensed cadres - Clarify what the workforce registry holds versus payroll and HR systems, and its privacy obligations - Govern national role terminology alongside international classifications and align with care-services discovery patterns - Consider sub-goals for identity and credentials, deployment and remuneration - Address purpose-limited reuse of workforce personal data and a suggested implementation sequence
