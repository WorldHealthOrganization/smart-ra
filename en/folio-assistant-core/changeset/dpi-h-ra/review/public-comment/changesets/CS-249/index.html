Require effective, not nominal, human oversight of AI
requirements
- Define assistive use by the system's actual role and influence - Describe human oversight as a behavioural and organisational capability, not just the presence of a reviewer - Articulate human-in-the-loop oversight principles - Consider a cross-cutting principle preserving transparency, accountability and override where automated processing materially affects people or institutions - Require proportionate exception and review where evidence is incomplete or conflicting - Require an operational oversight model proportionate to risk, addressing automation bias and review capacity - Address automation bias and over-reliance, especially in low-resource settings - Cover fairness, safety, transparency, explainability and lifecycle governance
