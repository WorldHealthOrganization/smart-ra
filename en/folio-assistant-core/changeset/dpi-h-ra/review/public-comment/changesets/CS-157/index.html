title
Broaden the licence and qualification model in the Workforce Registry
requirements
- Use a governed licence-status value set including not applicable, provisional, restricted and relicensed states - Separate formal qualifications, certifications, CPD and scope-of-practice authorisations - Model licences with scope, jurisdiction, conditions and non-fixed expiry - Support multiple licences and licences without expiry - Support multiple qualifications, with place of training (national/foreign) for each - Model qualifications and specialisations as repeatable, verified records with issuing authority - Record status reason, scope, jurisdiction and effective period separately - Make specialisation conditionally mandatory
