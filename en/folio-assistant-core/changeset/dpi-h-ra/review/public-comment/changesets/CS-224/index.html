title
Decompose cross-cutting enablers in the goal mappings
requirements
- Replace the single interoperability box with its underlying services (discovery, trust, routing, validation, semantics, mapping) - Replace a single consent or privacy box with distinct capabilities: legal basis, authorisation, consent/preference, audit, data rights - Split legal identity, health person identification, authentication, credentials, authorisation and record linkage - Make SGI and governed semantic artefacts (profiles, mappings, data dictionaries) visible where components depend on them - Show trust enablers (governance, security, legal authority, accountability) as explicit capabilities - Make the foundational DPI dependency list explicitly non-exhaustive and context-specific - Include provenance, units, status and timing as semantic requirements where clinical data is exchanged - Map purpose-specific minimum datasets and restricted access where goals reuse personal data
